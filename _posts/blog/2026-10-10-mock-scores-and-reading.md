Taking a drop year requires a specific kind of pacing. I spent the morning reviewing my latest CLAT mock scores and digging into the legal reasoning sections. The sheer volume of current affairs can feel overwhelming, but keeping the ultimate goal of NLUD in focus helps maintain momentum.

To decompress, I have been stepping away from the screen entirely in the evenings. I finally picked up _A Thousand Splendid Suns_ again. There is something profoundly grounding about Khaled Hosseini's prose that perfectly counterbalances the rigid logic required for entrance exams. It is a necessary reset before diving back into Indian constitutional history tomorrow.
