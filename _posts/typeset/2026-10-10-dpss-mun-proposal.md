# DPSS MUN: First Interschool Edition Proposal

Drafting a comprehensive structural framework for a Model United Nations conference requires meticulous attention to detail. This document serves as the official Proposal and Letter of Patronage for the inaugural interschool edition of DPSS MUN.

Rather than relying on ad-hoc planning, this document establishes a rigid structural framework. It codifies governance and decision rights, establishes "Fixed Points" for operations, and even implements mathematical algorithms for logistics, such as calculating delegate capacities and financial viability.[^1]
